Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: August 1, 2026

To request deletion of information associated with your interaction with new_bot, send an email to:

obelisk50_antique@icloud.com

What to include

  • Subject line: new_bot data deletion request;
  • your Instagram username;
  • a brief description of the interaction or data you want deleted;
  • any additional information reasonably needed to verify the request.

What happens next

We will review the request, verify it when necessary, and delete or anonymize data under our control unless retention is required by law, security needs, or legitimate technical obligations.

Response time

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 calendar days and complete valid requests within 30 calendar days, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

Meta and Instagram data

Some information may be controlled directly by Meta or Instagram. Requests concerning data held by those platforms should also be made through their own account and privacy tools.
